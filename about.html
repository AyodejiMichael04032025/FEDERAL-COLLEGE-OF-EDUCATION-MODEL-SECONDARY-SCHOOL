About Us (A Brief History Of the School)



The school was established in September 2002, and commenced with the 2002/2003 session by the proprietor, the provost of Federal College Of Education, Dr. J. O. Akinmoyewa. Academic activities began on October 2nd, 2002 with thirty six (36) students comprising of twenty four (24) J.S.S 1 students and twelve (12) J.S.S 2 students. The good leadership quality has led the school to have experienced different levels of achievements. In order to restategise the school for education excellence, the school moved to her permanent site on 1st November 2007. The school is enriched with highly diligent and committed members of staff. There are facilities such as well-equipped laboratories forpractical-based lessons, a good transportation system, boarding house system where students are given parental care; indeed it is a home away from home and a second home in ramification. We also have a library, a sick bay with qualified medical personnel, sporting equipment to mention but a few, The school has also turned out high performances in WAEC/NECO/JAMB, The likes of Shittu prisicilla 355/400(2023) in JAMB, Ojo Joy nine A1s in WAEC(2016), kuyoro Favour 360/400 in JAMB(2025)and lots more. The current proprietor DR. R.A.Soyele(provst of FCE Abeokuta)is always after the school's progress. To ensure quality management and progress, Dr. Adeniji(chief lecturer in Business Education Derpartment) was appointed as the Board of the school and the current Principal is Mr. Morfikoya.
